Common questions

Is home sample collection available?

Yes. Book on call, WhatsApp or the form above and our staff will visit your home at your chosen slot.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Some tests like Fasting Blood Sugar and Lipid Profile need 10–12 hours of fasting (water is fine). We'll tell you when you book.

How will I get my report?

Reports are sent on WhatsApp and email. You can also collect a printed copy from the centre.

How long do reports take?

Most routine tests are reported within 24 hours. Some special tests take longer; we'll share the expected time while booking.
